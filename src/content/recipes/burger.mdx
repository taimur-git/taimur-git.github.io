## Shopping

Supermarket: Agora, Meena Bazar, Shwapno, Unimart, Lavender. Meat from Bengal Meat or your kasai.

- **Beef mince, 560g, 20 to 25% fat.** Ask for it ground with chorbi. Local mince runs lean and lean
  mince makes a dry, grey patty. If they will not do it, buy beef and a piece of fat separately and
  have both ground together. This is the one thing worth being fussy about.
- **Beresta.** Buy a packet, or fry your own. Used twice.
- **Cheese slices, 8.** Plain processed. Two per burger.
- **Buns, 4.** Any shop burger bun. The soft slightly sweet local bakery ones are the best of them,
  close to a potato bun.
- **Jarred dill pickles.** Whole or spears are better than pre-sliced; you want to cut your own planks.
- **Khejur gur.** Winter item. Regular gur, or honey, if it is out of season.

## Equipment

A heavy pan, cast iron or a thick tawa. A heavy spatula or a flat press, something you can lean on.
A sharp knife.

## What does the work

Four things: beresta both in the seasoning and as a crisp layer in the burger, charred onion blitzed
through the sauce, gur in place of sugar, and buns toasted in browned butter.

## Notes

- The onion smash is not optional. Raw onion pressed into the meat steams, then fries in the beef
  fat, and fuses into the crust. It is the single biggest thing separating this from a normal burger,
  and it costs nothing.
- Two thin patties beat one thick one. Four browned faces instead of two, and that is where the
  flavour is.
- Do not crowd the pan. Two patties at a time, no more. Crowding drops the temperature and you steam
  the meat instead of searing it.
- Beresta twice is deliberate. Ground into the salt it seasons from the inside; scattered on top it
  is texture. Add it last so it stays crisp.
- The sauce is better after 30 minutes in the fridge, but usable straight away.

## If you need to cut further

- Skip the relish and use the pickle planks alone. Saves 3 minutes, loses a layer.
- Skip browning the butter and toast in plain butter. Saves 3 minutes, but it is a bad trade:
  browned butter is the cheapest flavour in the recipe.
- Skip the beef salt batch. Season the mince with salt, pepper, garlic powder, a crushed stock cube
  and MSG. Blitzed beresta stirred into the mince gets you most of the way back.

## On the nutrition estimate

The sauce is the swing factor. 50g of it per burger is about 355 kcal and 38g of fat, so using half
takes off roughly 175 kcal. Browned butter on the buns is another 110 kcal, and the two cheese slices
are 120.

A rough breakdown: patties 340 kcal, sauce 355, bun 160, butter 110, cheese 120, beresta and
vegetables the remaining 65.

Salt is high. The beef salt, cheese, pickles and bun come to roughly 2g of salt per burger before you
season the patties at the pan.
