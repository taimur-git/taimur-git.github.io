## Notes

- Taste before adding the sugar.
- Honey does not survive the simmer, so if you use it then use 1½ tsp stirred in off the heat with the lemon.
- Keeps 3 to 4 days in the fridge and is better on day two. Reheat gently. Freezes up to 3 months if you stop before the cream and add it on reheating.
