## Notes

- **Beef mince, 560g, 20 to 25% fat.** Ask for it ground with chorbi. Lean mince makes a dry, grey
  patty. If they will not do it, buy beef and a piece of fat separately and have both ground together.
- Two thin patties are better than one big one.
- Two patties in the pan at a time, no more. 
- The sauce is better after 30 minutes in the fridge, but usable straight away. It keeps a week; the
  beef salt keeps months in a jar.
- Patties can be shaped, stacked between paper and frozen up to 3 months. Cook from frozen, a minute
  longer a side. Cooked burgers do not reheat.
- If you need to cut further, skip the relish and use the pickle planks alone. Saves 3 minutes. Skip the beef salt batch. Season the mince with salt, pepper, garlic powder and MSG.
