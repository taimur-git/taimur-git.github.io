A stripped-down pandemic simulator that runs entirely in a terminal.

You play the pathogen. You start in one country, spread across a braille world map, spend DNA points on new traits, and try to finish the job before humanity finishes the cure.

It's relatively extensible so you can add whatever countries you want then rebake the map.
